Zodiac Twelve Directory Breach

The Zodiac Twelve keep their own directory, separate from the archive downstairs and from the guardian console before it — a strict, hierarchical roster with exactly one entry per member, and one seat, the chairman's, above the rest. Look up a member by uid and you get their public record. Sign in with a uid and password and, on a real match, you get their console.

Objective: this directory doesn't hand out its members' entries any way you like — look one up by name, or sign in as one, and that's all you get. Somewhere past the shape of an ordinary query is a wider one.
