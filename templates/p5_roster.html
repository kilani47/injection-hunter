The Royal Guard Rankings

The Palace keeps a public roster of the Royal Guard, and lets any visitor sort it however they like, by name, by division, by whatever column they name. The roster desk trusts the column you ask it to sort by completely; it was only ever expected to be the name of a column that already exists.

Objective: every column on this roster is public, there's nothing secret in it to read directly. But the order the desk hands rows back in is decided by an expression you control, and an expression can ask a question about something the roster never shows you.
