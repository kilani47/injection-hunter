The Spell Card

Every card on Greed Island is cast into the field the same way, win or lose: the game master reads the card, resolves its effect, and tells you nothing back except that it happened. This reader is no different. Whatever a card actually does, you will never see it in this page's own response: not the effect, not an error, not even a hint that something went wrong.

Objective: the game master isn't the only one watching the field. Something else is listening on a channel the visible response never touches. Find a way to make the card's true effect travel there instead, and read it off that channel.
