Finals-1

The tower's last floor is not one door but four, sunk one beneath the next. Each opens to a different kind of question, and the key that opens each door is sealed behind the door before it. Nothing here will tell you how a door opens, only you can find that out, by reading what the room beyond it is willing to say. Descend in order. There is no other way down.
