The Silent Courier

The Palace's courier service accepts dispatch orders as small XML documents: submit one and the desk confirms it was received, nothing more. The desk never reads any part of your document back to you, never reflects any element, any entity, anything at all. What the parser resolves internally stays internal.

Objective: the response tells you nothing. The parser's resolution of external entities is a network event, not a reflection event, and network events leave traces somewhere besides this response.

POST /p5/courier accepts a single form field, xml. The desk confirms receipt with a fixed response or rejects malformed XML. Send Accept: application/json for a compact JSON answer. Out-of-band activity, if any, is visible at GET /p5/courier/captures.
