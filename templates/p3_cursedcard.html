The Cursed Card

Not every card on Greed Island does something the moment it's drawn. Some sit quietly in the deck, inscribed, filed, entirely inert, until a later round, played by someone who never touched the round it was written in, wakes them up. The card you inscribe here today does nothing today. That's not a bug in the card.

Objective: get something you store now to have an effect later, in a different round, read by a different process, on a query you never typed a single character of yourself. The inscribing table below is genuinely safe to write to. The appraiser's report, on the far side of this page, is a separate matter entirely.
