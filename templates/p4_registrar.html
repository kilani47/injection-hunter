The Silent Directory

The registrar's verification desk checks one uid against one note and tells you only whether they match, by design, so no record's real contents are ever exposed through this particular door. No name, no title, no note text, win or lose.

Objective: yes or no is still an answer. Ask the registrar enough carefully-chosen yes/no questions about the same entry, and the silence starts to say more than it was ever supposed to.
