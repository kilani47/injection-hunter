Privacy Policy

This is a local security training lab. It has no server of its own on the internet, no account system, and no analytics. Here is exactly what it does with data, in full.

What runs where

Every part of this lab, the web portal and every database it talks to, runs inside Docker containers on your own machine. Nothing here is hosted by the author, and nothing you do on this site reaches the author or any third party.

The one cookie this site sets

The portal sets a single signed session cookie to remember which floors you have cleared, so the hub can show your progress and unlock the next node. That cookie holds a list of challenge ids you have solved and nothing else: no name, no email, no IP address, no device information. Visit /reset at any time to clear it, or clear cookies for this site in your browser.

No tracking, no analytics, no external requests

This site loads no analytics script, no advertising pixel, and no third-party font or asset. Rajdhani and IBM Plex Mono, the two typefaces used throughout, are bundled with the app under static/fonts/ rather than loaded from a font provider, so opening any page here makes no request to any server outside your own machine.

The one exception is entirely internal: Phase 3's out-of-band SQL injection lesson has you make the lab's own database call back to a small collaborator service, also running in your own Docker network. That callback never leaves your machine either.

What you enter into the challenges

Every challenge in this lab is a target you attack on purpose: forms, headers, and API bodies here store whatever you type into synthetic, disposable databases seeded fresh in your own containers. None of it is read by the author, sent anywhere else, or retained beyond your local Docker volumes. Running docker compose down -v deletes all of it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever needs to change, the update will land in this same file in the project's source history, same as any other change to the lab.
