A staff-only console for debugging failed incident-report searches. Unlike the front desk, this one doesn't hide its mistakes: whatever the database actually says when a query goes wrong, this console repeats back word for word.

Objective: a clerk who never swallows an error is still a clerk who was never supposed to answer the question you're about to ask. Find out what the archive says when it can't parse what you've handed it.
