Bypassing the Archive Guardian

A second door, deeper in the archive. This one doesn't just report a match/no-match bit like the front desk — it logs you in as whoever it decides you are. The guardian staffing it checks that a username and a password were both supplied. It was never told to check what kind of thing "supplied" actually means.

Objective: get past this login and into the console behind it, without ever knowing a single real agent's password.
