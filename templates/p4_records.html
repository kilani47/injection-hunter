Basic Records Room

The front desk of the Association's records archive. Give it a field and a value, and it searches, but it was never built to hand a caller anything back except whether something matched. No document, no field contents, no listing. One bit: matched, or it didn't.

Objective: the desk clerk answers exactly one question, over and over. That's still enough to walk a value out of the archive one character at a time, if you can get the clerk to compare something other than a plain word.
